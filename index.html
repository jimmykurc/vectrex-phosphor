Under the hood

A cycle-counted Motorola 6809, a VIA 6522 and the analog vector board — DAC, sample-and-hold mux, integrators, ZERO, BLANK and RAMP — modelled cycle by cycle. The beam is drawn into a WebGL phosphor buffer with persistence and bloom; the AY-3-8912 plays through an AudioWorklet.

The demo game is an original 6809 program assembled by a small assembler in this repo, so it runs without any BIOS. No ROMs are included.
